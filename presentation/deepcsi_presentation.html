The Challenge

The CSI Feedback Bottleneck

  • FDD Massive MIMO requires the UE to explicitly report downlink channel state to the gNodeB
  • A 32-antenna, 256-subcarrier array produces 8,192 complex coefficients per feedback report
  • That is 16,384 float values (~64 KiB) transmitted on every reporting interval
  • Overhead scales linearly with antenna count — more antennas means even more feedback

The Key Insight

Wireless channels in the angular-delay domain are naturally sparse. Most energy concentrates in a few multipath clusters. DeepCSI learns to extract only the essential structure, discarding redundancy that traditional methods transmit wastefully.

The Analogy

Think of it like sending a high-resolution photo over a slow connection. Instead of transmitting every pixel, our AI learns what the photo looks like and sends only a compact description. The receiver reconstructs the full image from that description.

Domain Theory

Angular-Delay Domain Sparsity

The Physics
  • Radio signals bounce off buildings, creating 3-8 multipath clusters
  • Each cluster arrives at a specific angle and delay
  • The 2D FFT maps to angular-delay, where this structure becomes sparse
  • Most of the 32 x 256 matrix is empty — energy concentrates in few hot spots
Why This Enables Compression

After truncating to 32 x 32 delay taps, we retain approximately 100% of channel energy while reducing the input by 8x. The remaining 2,048 scalars still have significant redundancy that our CNN encoder learns to eliminate.

Compression Pipeline Step-by-Step
1

  • Dedicated uplink: FDD guarantees uplink availability for latency-critical applications

  • Solves the problem where it exists: in FDD bands
  • No spectrum migration required — works with existing allocations
  • Complements TDD deployments by enabling hybrid FDD+TDD strategies
  • Aligned with 3GPP Rel-18 explicit focus on AI/ML for FDD CSI feedback

Definitions

Evaluation Metrics Explained

NMSE (dB)

Normalized Mean Squared Error in decibels. Measures reconstruction fidelity.

-38 dB means error is 0.015% of signal power. Lower is better.

NMSE = 10 log10( ||H - H'||2 / ||H||2 )
Beamforming Gain (G)

Normalized MRT Power Gain in [0, 1]. Measures practical signal quality.

0.9998 means 99.98% of max power reaches user. Higher is better.

G = |h'^H h|2 / (||h'||2 ||h||2)
Compression Ratio (CR)

Ratio of input to compressed size. CR=16 means 2048 scalars compressed to 128 scalars.

Higher CR = more compression, more bandwidth saved.

CR = Total Scalars / Latent Dim
